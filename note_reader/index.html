3 · NEXT STEP

Read a note to see the suggested next step.
Flags come from an LLM (model) reading the note against our taxonomy (technique_classifier/taxonomies/clinical_notes.yaml). Every flagged quote is checked against the original text; quotes not found in the note are dropped. Next-step percentages: chest X-ray sensitivity from Bradley et al. 2019 (systematic review, 77-80%); CT sensitivity is a simulator assumption; biopsy yields are measured in our pathway simulator on synthetic cases. Prototype decision support: a flag means "worth a second look", and a clinician confirms every step.
