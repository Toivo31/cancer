# Capacity vs order: evidence from other pathways (14 Nov 2026)

## Speeding up one step alone did not shorten the pathway
- UK NOLCP local rapid CT (ERJ 2023 abstract): CXR to CT fell from median 15 to 3 days, but abnormal CXR to treatment decision stayed 66 vs 65 days. Authors: other diagnostic and staging delays must also be addressed. https://publications.ersnet.org/content/erj/62/suppl67/pa1123
- Stokstad et al. 2019 (Norway): faster PET alone 40 -> 48% within 42 days (not significant); reordering 80%. https://link.springer.com/doi/10.1186/s12913-019-4517-z
- Radiotherapy DES review (Salford): more treatment-machine capacity may not improve throughput if an earlier step is rate-limiting. https://salford-repository.worktribe.com/OutputFile/2660286

## Reordering / redesign cut times
- Norway county hospital (Laerum et al. 2020): local diagnostic algorithm -7.8 days, national pathways -14.9 days time to treatment; more diagnostic procedures associated with longer time. https://core.ac.uk/download/479092742.pdf
- Toronto standardised triage with pre-ordered tests (JCO OP): referral to diagnosis 39.9 -> 28.1 days; pre-ordered PET 23.0 vs 35.9 days. https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7564135
- Royal Free London lung pathway: referral to treatment 66 -> 29 days; non-cancer answer 22 -> 12 days. https://pulsetoday.co.uk/?p=30344
- Southlake (Ontario) Lean: batching was main waste; volumes +65%, wait -60%. https://www.longwoods.com/content/26082
- Wales DES (England et al.): first appointment within 7 days + streamlined tests -> about 11 days less, +21% within 62-day target. https://www.pubmed.gov/36760877/
- Montpellier multidisciplinary workup at first visit (Pujol 2023): significantly faster histological diagnosis. https://doaj.org/article/be5fa10ccb474503a25308361debcf48
- Swansea rapid diagnosis centre for non-specific symptoms: 84 -> under 6 days if diagnosed at the visit; cost-effective at >= 80% utilisation. https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6960004

## Caveats
- Real capacity shortages exist (Royal Free imaging and endoscopy, Cooke et al. 2022, DES + particle swarm optimisation). https://discovery.ucl.ac.uk/10165787/1/1-s2.0-S277244252200034X-main.pdf
- Crowding out: fast tracks can lengthen waits for other patients (Sweden CPP interviews; London GP concern). https://link.springer.com/doi/10.1186/s12913-019-4413-6
- Equity angle: non-specific symptoms wait a median 34 days longer (Sewell); Scotland RCDS aims to narrow the gap between non-specific and site-specific presenters.
- Prior art: DES of cancer pathways and DES + optimisation already published. Our difference must be the decision model and revealed-information optimisation.
