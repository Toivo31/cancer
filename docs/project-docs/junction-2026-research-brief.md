## Core facts
- Fri 13 Nov 16:00 → Sun 15 Nov 18:00, Hype Areena, Kivenlahti, Espoo. Hacking 19:30 Fri → final submission Sun 10:00 (~38.5 h).
- €100k pool, €10k main prize. Per-challenge prizes are set by partners and not published yet.
- Challenge briefs revealed on the Junction Platform "closer to the event" and in the challenge videos at 19:10 Fri. Draft challenge pick due Fri 23:59 (changeable). A project can be submitted to multiple challenges.
- Rules: no code written before the event. A project related to an existing company is disqualified. Submissions are MIT-licensed (the team keeps ownership). 2-min video hard cap. Pitch deck PDF required. The whole team must attend peer review Sun 10:30–14:00 to be eligible for the main prize.
- Judging: partners pick challenge winners on their own criteria. Peer review → top 5 → 3-min pitch + 3-min Q&A → 7-judge jury. Guidebook criteria: Innovation, Impact, Implementation.
- Grand jury: Kia Kanninen (Founders House), Daksh Raghuvanshi (AfterQuery RL lead), Otto Söderlund (Supercell AI lead), Timo Ahopelto (Lifeline), Umar Noor (Google DeepMind), Hubertus van der Drift (Context.dev CTO), Arturs Polis (Verda CTO).
