# 03 · Queues in print

Cheerful dystopia: friendly shapes, an unfriendly situation. Pictograms, gouache, woodcut and cyanotype, as if a public-information office had drawn the waiting list.
Light board (cream `#F8F3E2` ground, teal `#376464` numerals).

| Image | Note |
|---|---|
| **10 · Queue to the tower** (`10-tower-queue.jpg`) | The cheerful-propaganda joke at full strength: smiling figures, a brutalist tower, a vignette that reads as found 80s video. The calm is the unsettling part. |
| **11 · The long way round** (`11-smiling-queue.jpg`) | The same idea in folded-paper gouache, warmer and less monumental: the clinic is a tiny tower on the horizon. A good mid-video interstitial. |
| **12 · Care pathway, split** (`12-care-pathway.jpg`) | Cream pictogram beside a blue cyanotype, with a hatched bottleneck. The closest thing to a before-and-after. The title text is clean; the small labels are not. |
| **13 · Rows that lengthen** (`13-pictogram-rows.jpg`) | The cleanest pure pictogram: identical figures, rows getting longer, red dots for the exceptions. Easy to redraw in code with real wait times. |
| **14 · The pointing hand** (`14-red-hand.jpg`) | The boldest bet: a constructivist red hand over a climbing crowd. Use sparingly, for the stern beat. |
| **15 · Entry, population, clinics** (`15-entry-population.jpg`) | People thin out toward the clinics beside a big clock. The tags and numbers are invented, so redraw them with real figures. |

**Section note.** Keep the menace in the length of the line and the face of the clock, never in the faces of the people or the staff. The humour does the work, so it can be a little cruel to the queue and kind to everyone in it.
