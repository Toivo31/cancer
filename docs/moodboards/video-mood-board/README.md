# Junction 2026 video mood board

"Retro-renaissance-recon": an 80s public-information film that turns into a product demo.
Live version (canvas, 5 artboards): https://claude.ai/artifact/K2kTsmKrjiBPs93PPRN3Mk

**Idea.** Waiting is a decision nobody made. Cancer patients in Finland wait longest not during the tests but between them, in queues. The platform maps the whole diagnostic pathway as one flowchart, recommends the next step at every point as a second opinion for the doctor, and, as steps become automated, makes decisions instantly across the whole system. It then replays the same simulated patients through redesigned pathways to find the order of steps and the fast tracks that shorten time to treatment. In a published Norwegian lung-cancer study, reordering the steps alone raised the share treated on time from 40% to 80%. A clinician approves every change.
