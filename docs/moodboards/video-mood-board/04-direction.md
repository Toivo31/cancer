# 04 · Direction

**Waiting is a decision nobody made.**
An 80s public-information film that turns into a product demo: unease first, then a calm, precise system, and a human who stays accountable.

## Look
Two worlds in one film. The past: 80s documentary, 4:3, VHS tracking, beige plastic, green phosphor. The present: teal-black, white hairlines, one amber accent. Renaissance as the bridge: engraved diagrams and recon boxes with decimal scores. People as silhouettes or seen from behind.

## Sound
- Act 1: old, sacred and bureaucratic (a baroque piece on synth, or choral), over stamps, ticks and printer noise, with a deadpan 80s narrator.
- One full second of silence at the turn.
- Act 2: the same sounds, now on the beat. Cold, quantised, elegant.

## Tone dials
- Dystopia: 3 out of 10, aimed at the queue.
- Techno-capitalist swagger: 2 out of 10.
- Humour: dry.
- Pace: slow and heavy, then fast and surgical.
- Text cards: five words or fewer.

## Guardrails
- Respect the doctors: provocative about the queue, never the clinicians.
- Say synthetic and simulated.
- Plain words on screen. Only claims with a source.
- No copyrighted footage in the cut.

The beat-by-beat script is in the project doc `trailer-script-and-prompt`.
