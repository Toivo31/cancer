# Deck: final content, 10 slides

> **Safe to claim / do not claim** (numbers from `numbers_sheet.md`; update there first)
>
> **Safe to claim**
> - All patients are synthetic. The Finnish county is a scenario, not calibrated to Finnish capacity data.
> - Norway, fitted: the observed pathway, [[s_obs_share]]% within 42 days (published [[s_pub_base]]%). This is the only thing fitted.
> - Norway, predicted and not fitted: the authors' reordered pathway is [[s_auth_share]]% at our best fit (published [[s_pub_opt]]%). Across all [[s_ext_n]] settings that fit the observed data it ranges from [[s_ext_auth_min]]% to [[s_ext_auth_max]]%, typically about [[s_ext_auth_med]]%; [[s_ext_auth_in_ci_pct]]% of the settings fall inside the published interval ([[val_pub_opt_ci]]%).
> - The ranking holds in all [[s_ext_rank_n]] settings: reordering beats a faster scanner beats today.
> - We over-predict the faster scanner alone: [[s_fast_share]]% at the best fit against the published [[s_pub_fast]]% (range [[s_ext_fast_min]] to [[s_ext_fast_max]]%).
> - Optimiser: [[o_txt_core]]. With the faster scanner (like for like with the authors): [[o_txt_fast]]. With no new equipment: [[o_txt_nof]]. It chose from a menu of about [[s_candidates]] single changes that contained the authors' changes.
> - Finland, synthetic: two reorders, no new equipment, [[f_share_base]]% to [[f_share_opt]]% treated within [[f_target]] days (provisional target) on two held-out cohorts; the gain is [[r_f_gain_lo]] to [[r_f_gain_hi]] points across [[r_f_cohorts]] cohorts.
> - The primary-care loop is a mechanism with placeholder decision tables. The patient engine makes synthetic cards; the catalogue is a draft no clinician has read.
>
> **Do not claim**
> - "We predicted 80% exactly", "it matches the paper", or "78 to 86%" (the older, narrower grid).
> - That the optimiser "beat" or "improved on" the authors, or "discovered their answer from nothing" (its menu contained their changes); that its lead over our simulated authors' pathway is a finding (it is not, see slide 6); that the fourth change is safe or the source of any lead.
> - "Reordering with no new equipment beats buying the scanner" without the count: [[o_txt_scanner]]. Never quote [[s_opt_share]]% or [[s_noeq_share]]% without the range.
> - [[f_cond_share_100]]% as a Finnish result. It is conditional on assumed urgent booking times.
> - Any Finnish number as an estimate for real Finnish patients or counties, or any lives saved.
> - Any number from the primary-care loop as a Finnish estimate. The loop uses placeholder decision tables and every behaviour number is an assumption; [[e_framing]]. Do not use the old figures (37 days, one cancer in six, 84 days, 840 EUR).
> - That a model trained on synthetic cards is validated on real patients, or that the team's decision model (JEV) produced the headline numbers (placeholder tables and rules did).
> - That the equity gap closes (in points it widens, [[equity_gap_base_pp]] to [[equity_gap_opt_pp]]), or that faster care saves lives in lung cancer.
> - That the model-written cards are "leak-free" or clinically realistic. The review found report wording in the old result prompt that gives away false alarms; [[lib_g2_sentence]]. Patient backgrounds come from a population model whose numbers are mostly assumptions.
> - That the paper's text gives 80% exactly: [[o_txt_literal]].

## Speaker notes, one line each

1. Number, then the change, then stop. Say "in our synthetic county".
2. These are reported figures from the dashboard, not ours.
3. Say that most of the time is waiting, and that primary care is the biggest single stage in our model.
4. The 80% is a model estimate in the paper; the lesson is direction (ranking), not the exact size. Our capacity-only control is the same extra capacity with the old order. Add the PET-at-triage caveat when asked about 80%.
5. Do not name the architecture. A judge can ask; see qa.md.
6. Say the band, not just the best fit: "80 at our best fit, 62 to 86 across every setting that fits the data, about 75 typically." Say the miss: we over-predict the faster-scanner-only result (60 against 48). Say that the ranking holds in every setting. Never say "exactly".
7. Held-out numbers, not training numbers. Say "two reorders, no new equipment"; if asked about [[f_cond_share_100]]%, explain it is conditional on assumed urgent booking times.
8. Report both gap measures, as what the simulation shows under its assumptions; do not say the gap closes.
9. Actions 4 and 5 are not tested by us.
10. Synthetic, stated plainly, with a path to real data. If asked about the patient cards: "500 template-written and a few dozen model-written, all synthetic; we do not claim they behave like real patients."
