## Spoken text only

In our synthetic Finnish county, 18 percent of lung cancer patients start treatment within 90 days of first contact. With two changes to the pathway, 61 percent. All patients are simulated, and we say so.

Most of the delay is waiting, not testing: 87 percent of the days. The biggest block is in primary care, about 30 days before a referral.

Our optimiser proposes changes using only what each step revealed. It reruns the same patients to accept a change, and we report on patients it never saw. It suggests one: treat every suspected-cancer referral as urgent once the hospital has handled it. It rejected a second idea, GP instead of nurse at first contact, because more cancers were missed.

We checked it against a real Norwegian hospital first. Fitted only to the observed pathway, it reproduces 40 percent on time, and predicts 80 percent for the authors' pathway. They published 80. It overshoots on a faster scanner alone: 60 against 48. Searching blind, it finds the authors' three changes plus one, and with no new equipment reaches 67 percent. Order beats capacity.

Both groups gain: vague symptoms from 13 to 54 percent, alarm symptoms from 27 to 79. The gap in days narrows, the gap in shares does not. How big that gap is, is our assumption.

Our proposal: order tests together at the first specialist visit, pair direct scans with it, fast-track vague symptoms with published safeguards, monitor waits by county, and pilot in one county first. A doctor decides every case.

## Claims to check before recording

- "Missed cancers do not rise": held-out 27.5 to 27.5 per cohort in the current bundle; recheck after any rerun.
- "Biggest block in primary care": the largest stage in the current bundle is detection at 30 days; if a rerun changes this, change the sentence. (An earlier draft said the longest queue sat between referral and diagnosis; the run does not show that.)
- "The gap in days narrows, in shares does not": narrows and widens in this bundle; flip the wording if a rerun reverses either.
- "Finds the authors' three changes plus one": the fourth is booking tissue sampling at the first visit, which needs a clinical check (D18); drop "plus one" if the team removes it.
- Word count of spoken text is about 250; trim the validation paragraph first if the read runs over two minutes.
