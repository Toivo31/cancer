Why we trust the engine

We rebuilt a published Norwegian pathway, fitted only the observed order, and let the engine predict the reordered one. It matches the authors' estimate, with a wide range, and over-states the faster-scanner-only gain. We show both.

Synthetic patients: two ways of writing the same patient

Every patient starts as a hidden set of facts: what is truly wrong, and what each test would show. A language model, or a plain template, turns those facts into the words a clinician would meet. The decision model only ever sees the words. It never sees the hidden facts.

Read these with the numbers. Synthetic data: no real patient is used anywhere. Primary-care behaviour is assumed: the encounter loop's decision rates are tuned to plausible practice, not measured in Finland.
