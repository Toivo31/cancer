The encounter loop

At each clinical decision a model scores the allowed next steps from the visible record; a person decides.

Live when the GPU is up, saved examples otherwise, always labelled as which. Scores are relative to the offered options, not probabilities.

Read these numbers with four caveats.

  • Synthetic data. No real patient is used anywhere.
  • Primary-care behaviour is assumed. The encounter loop's decision rates are tuned to plausible practice, not measured in Finland.
  • The Norway check supports the ranking of reorderings, not exact magnitudes.
  • Decision-model scores are relative candidate scores, not probabilities.
